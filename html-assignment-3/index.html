The Importance of Sleep

Getting enough sleep is very important for your health. It helps your body recover, refresh, and stay focused throughout the day.

A doctor once said, Good sleep is one of the best medicines.

Sleep is not a luxury. It is a necessity for the human body and mind. Getting enough rest improves memory, concentration, and overall well-being.

REM sleep is an important stage of sleep that helps with learning and memory.

Experts say we need at least 6 8 hours of sleep every night.
